    Shaurya Pratap Singh

    Currently, I am a Pre-Doctoral Fellow in the SPARKS Program at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc) , working under Prof. Sanjit Chatterjee. I work at the intersection of cryptography, protocol design, and post-quantum security. My current research is centered on building a Quantum-Safe Hybrid TLS Protocol. The goal is to combine classical and quantum-safe cryptographic primitives in a way that preserves compatibility with existing internet infrastructure while improving resilience against future adversaries equipped with quantum computing capabilities.

    My work spans protocol analysis, cryptographic primitive selection, and implementation trade-off evaluation. I am especially interested in how hybrid schemes can be designed to remain secure, efficient, and deployable in real-world systems, particularly in the context of TLS, key exchange, and authenticated communication. This program has given me a strong foundation in both theoretical cryptography and practical systems security, and it continues to shape my research direction.

    Academic Interests

    My academic interests span Cryptography, Post-Quantum Cryptography, Pseudorandomness, Quantum Computing and related areas of theoretical computer science, including complexity theory, coding theory, and computational number theory .
